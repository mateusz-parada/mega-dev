#4471 was filed as one bug: dates parse month first in eu locales. The reports hold three different problems. Only the first is the parser.

One ticket hid two bugs. Fixing the parser will not stop the form or the one-day move.

Problems

  1. parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so day-first tenants get flipped dates (7 April → 4 July). Reports come through the app and email.
  2. hint: tenants on the booking-v3 form (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour) see MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type month first and the form reads it their way. The parser fix does not touch this: change the form hint.
  3. one-day-move: dates are off by exactly one day, not a month flip. This looks like a timezone or DST problem. It needs its own investigation.

How we counted

  • The export repeats rows: one id, one report (15 duplicates removed).
  • Intake's intakeTag is a guess from the browser. We placed each report by its tenant's locale and booking form instead.
  • Reports are counted where they are filed, as the desk's own view does: 379 parser + 33 hint = 412. The five reports in desk-notes.json (oneDayMove) are filed under parser and are also the whole of problem 3, so they appear in both rows.
